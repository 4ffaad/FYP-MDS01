topic: MDS01 EEG Review and Video Privacy UX
updated: 2026-08-26T18:46
---

- (decision) The product has two peer frontend destinations: EEG Review and Video Privacy. They share the application shell but do not share a processing journey.
- (decision) EEG Review exposes the complete reviewed research pipeline: upload, validation, de-identification, preprocessing, inference, explanation, and results. It must show stage status and research-only output.
- (decision) Video Privacy is a standalone privacy-only workflow: upload video, choose one or more privacy profiles, process, preview/download the protected output, and inspect privacy quality flags. It does not call the EEG model or show clinical predictions.
- (decision) Initial video privacy profiles are pose-only and face-redacted. The UI must describe them as privacy transforms, not clinical/action analysis.
- (decision) Patient video and EEG are treated as separate inputs and separate sessions in the frontend. The first video experience must not require an EEG session.
- (decision) Patient-identifying media remains local/private and is never represented as sample content in the repository or UX mocks.
- (assumption) The primary form factor is responsive web, with desktop/laptop as the primary review surface and mobile/tablet support for upload, status, and output review.
- (assumption) The existing root DESIGN.md is the visual source of truth for the UX run; the new UX DESIGN.md will normalize its tokens into the BMad DESIGN.md contract without changing the product's visual direction.
- (decision) For v1, each Video Privacy job selects exactly one privacy profile. Profile composition is deferred until backend composition semantics are reviewed.
- (decision) Video UI uses generated non-identifying labels such as Video upload 01 or a short job ID. Client filenames, patient names, filesystem paths, and embedded identifiers are never rendered.
- (decision) The first video preview is a representative frame from the transformed output. The original video is never previewed in the result surface; full protected playback can be added later.
- (decision) A usable output with quality caveats is marked Needs review and requires explicit review acknowledgement before download. Outputs that fail validation remain unavailable.
- (decision) Retention and download expiry are backend-provided policy values. If policy is absent, the UI shows that retention is not configured and keeps download disabled.
- (change) Validation findings were applied: visual and behavioral component names are being normalized, contrast/focus requirements are explicit, and secondary surface states are included.
- (assumption) The backend can provide a non-identifying job label, transformed representative frame, output usability, quality flags, and retention/expiry metadata without exposing original identifiers.
- (event) spines finalized
